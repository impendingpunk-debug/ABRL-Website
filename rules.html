League Rulebook

Rules

Simple race-night rules built around clean racing, predictable Wednesday nights, and letting people actually race.

ScheduleStandingsResultsRules

League Basics

Mini Stocks, fixed setups, asphalt oval racing. ABRL is open to drivers who fit the league culture and race respectfully.

Race Night

Practice opens at 8:30 PM ET. Qualifying is two laps. Green flag is scheduled for 9:00 PM ET. Feature length is set in laps for each track.

Cautions & Repairs

Full-course cautions are enabled and caution laps count. One fast repair is available. There is no Lucky Dog.

Fuel & Tires

Fuel and tires are unlimited. The goal is to keep strategy straightforward and avoid turning short-track races into pit-stop contests.

Incident Limits

12x: Drive-through penalty.
16x: Disqualification.

Championship

Season 1 consists of 10 rounds. A driver's best 8 results count toward the championship. Two rounds may be dropped. No bonus points and no playoff system.

League Culture

Race hard, race clean, and respect the people around you. Contact can happen in short-track racing; reckless driving and drama are different things.

Race Direction

League administration may review incidents, apply penalties, and make common-sense decisions when something falls outside the written rules.
