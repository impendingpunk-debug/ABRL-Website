League Rulebook

Rules

The race-night rules and format in one place.

League Basics

Mini Stock. Fixed setup. Asphalt oval only. C-Class Oval license or higher. Maximum field of 43 cars.

Race Night

Practice opens at 8:25 PM ET. Two-lap qualifying begins at 8:55 PM ET. Green flag is at 9:00 PM ET. Feature length is 35 minutes.

Cautions & Repairs

Full-course cautions are enabled and caution laps count. One fast repair is available. There is no Lucky Dog.

Fuel & Tires

Fuel and tires are unlimited. The intent is to keep strategy simple and prevent the race from turning into a pit-stop contest.

Incident Limits

12x: Drive-through penalty.
16x: Disqualification.

Championship

Season 1 consists of 10 rounds. A driver's best 8 results count toward the championship. Two rounds may be dropped. There are no bonus points and no playoff system.

League Culture

Race hard, race clean, and respect the drivers around you. ABRL is designed to be parent-friendly, drama-free, and competitive without becoming a second job.
