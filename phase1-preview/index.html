What ABRL Is

Built around real life.

ABRL is competitive without pretending everyone has unlimited time. We race late enough to handle family life, keep the format moving, and expect people to respect the drivers around them.

After Bedtime

Family and real life come first. Race night starts after the important stuff is handled.

Short & Competitive

Lap-based features keep the racing meaningful without turning the night into a marathon.

Race With Respect

Door-to-door racing is encouraged. Driving through somebody because you can is not.

The Championship
